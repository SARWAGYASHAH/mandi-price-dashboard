science Methodology

1 Data Cleaning

Standardizes headers, filters target crops, parses dates and prices, removes invalid ranges, deduplicates records, and canonicalizes state names (e.g., Tamilnadu → Tamil Nadu). Derived features include season, rolling average, volatility flag, and anomaly flag. All decisions logged to cleaning_report.json.

2 SQL Analytics

Eight business-oriented queries cover state and commodity averages, top/bottom markets, monthly crop aggregation, season comparison, anomaly records, state rankings, and year-over-year change. All results exported as CSV for downstream consumption.

3 Dashboard Design

Four-page Power BI report: National Overview (KPIs, trends), State Comparison (India filled map), Crop Deep Dive (rolling averages, seasonal profiles), and Market Intelligence (anomaly investigation). 18 DAX measures with documented date model and star schema.

4 Anomaly Detection & Forecasting

Z-score analysis flags 16,336 records exceeding two standard deviations. Prophet forecasts Onion and Tomato with strict chronological holdout — no future data leakage. Both models report high MAPE transparently as analytical baselines, not trading signals.
