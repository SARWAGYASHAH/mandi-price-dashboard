Time Series Forecasting

Useful baseline, honest uncertainty.

Prophet is evaluated chronologically and forecasts the national daily median modal price. High errors are reported as model limitations, not polished away.

account_tree Methodology Pipeline

1

Aggregate

Market quotes → national daily median modal price per commodity

2

Fill Gaps

Calendar-complete time axis with time-based interpolation

3

Configure

Prophet with weekly + yearly seasonality, multiplicative mode

4

Evaluate

Chronological holdout — last 90 days for Onion, 31 for Tomato

5

Refit

Train on all observations, produce final 90-day forecast

6

Export

CSV results + publication-quality PNG forecast charts

Honest Uncertainty
